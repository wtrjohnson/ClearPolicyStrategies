Why Clear Policy Strategies

What Makes Clear Policy
Strategies Different

Clear Policy Strategies is not a generalist lobbying firm. It's a boutique practice built around one discipline: translating complex tax and trade policy into real results for clients who need results, not reports.


Aaron Taylor spent twenty years working inside the Senate Finance Committee, the Trump transition, the Ways and Means Committee, and the Executive Branch, with active relationships at Treasury, Commerce, USTR, State, and the Senate Republican Conference.


Most firms offer access. Clear Policy Strategies offers access plus the policy depth to use it. Aaron has personally driven legislative outcomes worth hundreds of billions of dollars.

What Clients Get

Clients get direct, senior attention on every engagement, not a partner who hands the work to junior staff. Whether it's Section 232 and Section 301 tariff exposure, OECD-driven tax frameworks, or a position before a congressional committee, clients get direct strategic counsel on how Washington actually works.


Aaron's network covers twenty-five embassies, Senate Republican leadership, and key decision-makers across the current Administration, built over two decades. For companies that need someone who can get in the room and deliver, Clear Policy Strategies is the firm.
