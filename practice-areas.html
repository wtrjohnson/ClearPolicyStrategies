Clear Policy Strategies advises investors, companies, and foreign governments navigating U.S. tax, trade, and regulatory policy. Two focused practice areas — built from twenty years at the intersection of private equity and federal policy.

Practice Area One

Investment, Tax &
Capital Strategy

Tax law is written in Washington, but its impact is felt on term sheets, deal structures, and investment returns. When the rules are changing — or when you need to influence how they change — you need someone who understands both sides of the equation.

Aaron spent twenty years in private equity and federal tax policy. He knows how capital moves and how the legislation governing it actually gets made. Clear Policy Strategies gives you strategic counsel and direct access before decisions are finalized, not after.

  • Tax policy impact assessments for investment structures and M&A transactions
  • International tax structure advisory — GILTI, BEAT, FDII, and Pillar Two frameworks
  • Legislative monitoring on carried interest, capital gains, and financial services regulation
  • Advocacy before Senate Finance Committee and House Ways & Means Committee
  • Coalition building and stakeholder strategy for regulatory outcomes
  • Strategic counsel on CFIUS and foreign investment review processes

Practice Area Two

International Business &
Market Access

Washington is complicated for foreign governments and international businesses. The rules are fragmented, the politics move fast, and misreading a policy signal can be costly.

Aaron spent years at the Global Business Alliance advising nearly 200 international companies on U.S. tax, trade, and regulatory matters. He has active relationships at Treasury, Commerce, USTR, and across the Senate Republican Conference — and knows how to navigate the institutions that shape U.S. trade and investment policy.

  • Market entry strategy and U.S. regulatory roadmapping for foreign businesses
  • Tariff and trade agreement strategy — Section 232, Section 301, and bilateral negotiations
  • Advocacy before Treasury, Commerce, and the U.S. Trade Representative's office
  • Foreign government advisory on bilateral trade and investment frameworks
  • Political risk assessment and legislative intelligence for cross-border transactions
  • Stakeholder engagement with the National Economic Council and Domestic Policy Council
