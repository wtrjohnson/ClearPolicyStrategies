Aaron Taylor Founder, Clear Policy Strategies
Biography

Twenty Years on Both Ends
of the Conversation.

Aaron Taylor founded Clear Policy Strategies after twenty years split between federal policy and private equity — Senate Finance, the White House, and the executive branch on one side; managing legislative strategy for nearly 200 international companies on the other.

He started on the Senate Finance Committee under Chairman Orrin Hatch, running more than 30 hearings on tax reform. He got to know the Members and staff who write every major piece of tax and trade legislation — and how they actually think about it.

As VP of Tax & Trade at the Global Business Alliance, he lobbied on behalf of nearly 200 international companies — at Treasury, the White House, and Capitol Hill — on tax, trade, and financial services policy.

Over the past five years, he has focused on international clients: governments and companies working to invest or operate in the United States, using the same relationships at the NEC, Commerce, and USTR.

Clear Policy Strategies is a small practice by design. Clients value the relationships, but the deeper advantage is judgment: knowing who matters, what they care about, and when to engage.

Carlos A. Moreno
Salt Lake County Councilman

Carlos A. Moreno is a Salt Lake County Councilman and attorney who immigrated to the United States from Venezuela. He holds a law degree from Rafael Urdaneta University with a specialization in Venezuelan Labor Law, and earned degrees in Political Science & Government and Homeland Security & Emergency Management from Salt Lake Community College. A former student body president and Vice President of the Utah Student Association, Carlos brings a firsthand understanding of democratic institutions, governance, and the stakes of policy — both abroad and at home.
