Aaron Taylor Founder, Clear Policy Strategies
Biography

The Rare Combination of
Policy and Private Markets.

Aaron Taylor founded Clear Policy Strategies to bring rare, dual-track experience to clients navigating Washington — twenty years spanning private equity investing and federal policy advocacy, with deep relationships across Capitol Hill, Treasury, and the White House.

He began his political career on the Senate Finance Committee under Chairman Orrin Hatch, organizing over 30 hearings on tax reform and building direct relationships with the Members and staff who draft every major piece of tax and trade legislation. That foundation gave Aaron an insider's understanding of how policy is actually made — and how to influence it effectively.

As VP of Tax & Trade at the Global Business Alliance, he represented nearly 200 international companies before Treasury, the White House, and Capitol Hill — developing a uniquely global perspective on U.S. economic policy and the legislative environment that shapes cross-border investment.

Over the past five years, Aaron has focused on helping international governments and companies invest and succeed in the United States — bringing that same network to bear across the National Economic Council, Domestic Policy Council, Commerce Department, and the U.S. Trade Representative's office.

Today, Clear Policy Strategies serves a select group of clients who need more than access — they need strategic counsel from someone who has sat on both sides of the table.
