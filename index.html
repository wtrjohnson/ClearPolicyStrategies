What Makes Clear Policy
Strategies Different

Clear Policy Strategies is not a generalist lobbying firm. It's a boutique practice built around one discipline: translating complex tax and trade policy into real results for clients who need results, not reports.


Aaron Taylor spent twenty years working inside the Senate Finance Committee, the Trump transition, the Ways and Means Committee, and the Executive Branch, with active relationships at Treasury, Commerce, USTR, State, and the Senate Republican Conference.


Most firms offer access. Clear Policy Strategies offers access plus the policy depth to use it. Aaron has worked directly on tax, trade, and regulatory decisions affecting hundreds of billions of dollars in investment, cross-border commerce, and business planning.
